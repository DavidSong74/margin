Margin

Carry every journal you've ever kept.

Margin turns your physical, handwritten journals into a private, searchable digital archive. Snap quick photos of your old pages, and Margin uses AI handwriting recognition to transcribe them into a beautiful interface you can revisit anytime — while always keeping the original photo of your handwriting.

The problem

People who journal accumulate notebooks over the years — and then can't do anything with them. You can't carry a decade of journals with you. The boxes of old notebooks sit in a closet, and everything you ever wrote is effectively locked away: impossible to browse on the go, and impossible to search. Years of your own thoughts, memories, and reflections become unreachable simply because they're trapped on paper.

Why I built it

I wanted a way to keep all of my journals with me without physically carrying them. The idea is simple: invest a little time photographing pages you've already written, and in return get a digital version of every journal you own — one you can open on your phone, read through, and actually search. Instead of your writing gathering dust, it becomes something you can return to and rediscover.

How it solves the pain point
Digitize by photo, not by retyping. You just photograph a page; the AI does the transcription. Turning years of paper journals into a digital archive costs minutes, not hours.
Access everything, anywhere. Once a journal is in Margin, you no longer need the physical book. Your whole collection lives in your pocket.
Search across your entire life of writing. Every transcribed page becomes searchable, so you can instantly find any memory, person, or theme across years of journals — something a shelf of paper notebooks can never do.
Keep the original, always. The transcription is a searchable layer over your actual handwriting; the original photo is preserved as the source of truth, so nothing is lost.
Accuracy you can trust. Anything the AI is unsure about is flagged for your review rather than silently guessed, and your corrections teach it to read your handwriting better over time.
Key functionalities
Secure sign-in — private account with email/password or Google/Apple sign-in.
Journal library — all your journals as a shelf of book-like covers, each with a custom name and a cover image (chosen from the web or photographed from your real journal).
Photo capture — take or upload a picture of a handwritten page.
AI handwriting transcription — the page photo is transcribed by a vision AI model, faithfully preserving your exact words, spelling, and layout.
Confidence-flagged review queue — low-confidence words are surfaced for you to approve or correct, instead of being guessed at. Your corrections feed a personal glossary that improves future recognition of your handwriting.
Page reader — swipe through a journal's pages in a calm read mode, with a toggle to view either the clean transcription or the original photographed page.
Edit mode — turn editing on to fix transcriptions by keyboard, or add new pages by photo.
Full-text search — search across every journal at once to resurface past writing.
Export & backup — download your journals (e.g. as PDF) so your data is always yours.
Privacy first — journals are deeply personal, so data is handled with encryption and clear, transparent controls.
How it works, end to end

Take a photo of a page → the image is sent to a secure backend that calls the AI model → the model returns the transcribed text plus a confidence rating for each part → clean text is stored alongside the original image, and uncertain parts go to your review queue → you confirm or fix them, and those corrections make the next transcription smarter.
